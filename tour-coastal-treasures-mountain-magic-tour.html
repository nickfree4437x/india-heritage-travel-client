Cochin – Historic Heritage & Coastal Culture of Kerala

Cochin, is former name of Kochi, popularly known as the Queen of the Arabian Sea, is a busy commercial hub rich in spice trading. It is the centre of world spice trade for many centuries. It is emerging cosmopolitan city with busy life on streets, on the other hand it is also financial, commercial & industrial capital of Kerela. Its great to see that Kochi is the only city to have underwater metro system which is world's largest electric boat metro transportation infrastructure.

While you travel to Fort Kochi, you'll feel a different time period. The fort is settled into history & culture through ages. Kochi is a place with beautiful monuments & variety of linguistic & ethenic community such as Arabian, Dutch, Phoenician, Portuguese, Chinese, and British influences.

Alleppey – Kerala Backwaters & Coastal Heritage

Alleppey, officially known as Alappuzha, famous among the travellers is also known as "Venice of the East". The is an immensely beautiful town, embraced by Arabian Sea in the west & many lakes, lagoons & freshwater rivers. Alleppey offers a unique experience with lush paddy fields, traditional houseboats in serene water & coconut groves.

This town is also famous for its Nehru Trophy Boat Race, where tourists come across the globe. This town has rich cultural heritage & natural beauty, which makes it a must visit destination for travellers which assures peace of mind amidst nature's beauty.

Thekkady – Wildlife, Periyar Lake & Spice Plantations

Located in the Idukki district of Kerela, Thekkady is a beautiful hill station adorned with lush greenery, amazing landscapes & diverse wildlife. Here is the famous Periyar National Park, which is a sanctuary that has variety of animals which are even exotic in their natural habitat. The beautiful Periyar lake which is located within the park have the facilities of boat cruises which offers travellers unique experience to watch wildlife along the water edges.

The spice plantation of Thekkady is another famous spot where one can explore & learn more about cultivation of spices such as pepper, vanilla & cardamom. Along with these it has variety of outdoor activity too like trekking, nature walks, bamboo rafting. Thekkady is a heaven for adventure enthusiast as well as nature lover.

Munnar – Tea Gardens & Hill Station Beauty of Kerala

Munnar, the mesmerising hill-station in the Western Ghats of Kerala, is famous for its amazing rolling hills, cool climate, beautiful valleys & tea plantation. Also referred to as the "Kashmir of South India", it's a place of natural beauty. Located along the banks of three beautiful rivers – Madupetti, Nallathanni, and Periavaru, Munnar also have vast tea plantations & natural bird sanctuaries. Here you can also explore the tea museums & factories to learn more about the process of tea production. It have a rich biodiversity & breath-taking scenery, makes it a perfect getaway for trekkers & nature lovers.

Mumbai – Gateway of India & Cultural Heritage

Mumbai, the "City of Dreams", is a bustling financial capital of India & also the largest city in the country. The location of it along the Arabian Sea makes it a beautiful city with diverse culture, fast-paced life & historic landmark.

The most famous iconic landmark Gateway of India, & Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Terminus, is also a UNESCO World Heritage site & architectural marvel. The most famous Marine Drive also called as "Queen's Necklace" due to its twinkling night lights & another charm of Mumbai is Juhu beach provides an endless experience.

Detailed Itinerary

Day 1–3: Cochin

When you arrive at Cochin Airport, our team will warmly welcome you with a signboard and assist you with a smooth start to your South India journey.

For those arriving early, you can explore Kochi through a local sightseeing tour. Begin with St. Francis Church, one of the historic landmarks of Fort Kochi. Then visit the Paradesi Synagogue, one of the oldest active synagogues in the Commonwealth of Nations, followed by Mattancherry Palace, a Portuguese-built palace popularly known as the Dutch Palace.
Overnight stay will be at a hotel in Kochi.

Next stop: Alleppey

A 1-hour and 20-minute drive, covering 55 kilometres.

Day 3–4: Alleppey

Reach Alleppey by noon and check in to a beautiful houseboat. Enjoy a boat cruise through mesmerising Vembanad Lake and experience the peaceful backwaters of Kerala.

As you travel along the narrow strips of land, you can witness the daily lives of local fishing communities, women engaged in their routines, and children enjoying the backwaters. You will also see beautiful villages and landscapes along the way.

Next stop: Thekkady

A scenic 3-hour and 30-minute drive, covering 140 kilometres.

Day 4–6: Thekkady

In the early morning, you can enjoy a guided jungle hike and bamboo rafting experience in the Periyar region. The experience takes around 6 hours and offers an opportunity to explore the area's natural surroundings. Wildlife sightings may include elephants and other species found in the region.

You can also explore the fragrant spice markets, featuring spices such as cinnamon, pepper, and cardamom.

Venture into the fragrant spice plantations, where cardamom, cinnamon, pepper, and coffee thrive.

In the evening, you can enjoy a Kathakali dance performance, a traditional classical dance form of Kerala.

Next stop: Munnar

A 3-hour drive, covering 90 kilometres to Munnar.

Day 6–8: Munnar

As you enter Munnar, you can enjoy views of its extensive tea-growing landscapes. After reaching Munnar, you can experience a Kalaripayattu show, an ancient martial art that originated in Kerala.
The following morning, you can join a guided trekking experience covering approximately 9 km through grasslands, tea plantations, and rocky landscapes.

Next, visit the Munnar Tea Museum, which preserves the history and development of tea plantations in the region.

You can also visit Pothamedu Viewpoint, a scenic spot offering panoramic views of the surrounding valleys. Other nearby attractions include the Muthirapuzha River and Idukki Dam, with impressive views of the surrounding hills.

Next stop: Mumbai

A 2-hour flight to Mumbai.

Day 8–9: Mumbai

Explore the vibrant city of Mumbai and visit the Gateway of India, one of its most famous landmarks. Continue to the Taj Mahal Palace Hotel and Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Terminus, a UNESCO World Heritage Site known for its Victorian Gothic architecture.
You can also visit Mani Bhavan, Haji Ali, and a Jain temple. Dharavi can also be explored as part of Mumbai's diverse urban landscape and cultural experiences.

Day 10: Farewell & Departure

After discovering and exploring India and creating beautiful memories, you will be ready for your journey back home. Your dedicated driver will ensure you reach the airport on time according to your flight schedule. Safe travels!
