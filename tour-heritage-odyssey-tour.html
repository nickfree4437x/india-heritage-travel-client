Delhi – History, Heritage & Culture

Discover Delhi, a fascinating blend of history and modernity. Explore Old Delhi's vibrant streets, historic landmarks, Mughal-era architecture, and cultural experiences while discovering the heritage of India's capital.

Jaipur – The Pink City & Royal Heritage

Jaipur, the Pink City and heart of Rajasthan, is renowned for its grand forts, magnificent palaces, historic landmarks, and vibrant bazaars. Discover the city's rich royal heritage and traditional Rajasthani culture.

Agra – Taj Mahal & Mughal Heritage

Agra brings India's Mughal heritage to life through magnificent monuments, historic forts, and vibrant marketplaces. Discover the iconic Taj Mahal, a timeless symbol of love and one of India's most celebrated heritage landmarks.

Ajanta & Ellora- An Ancient Canvas Carved In Rock

The Ajanta and Ellora Caves are UNESCO World Heritage Sites renowned for their ancient rock-cut architecture, intricate sculptures, and remarkable cave paintings. Together, they showcase India's extraordinary artistic and architectural heritage.

Cochin – Colonial Heritage & Cultural Traditions

Cochin, is former name of Kochi, popularly known as the Queen of the Arabian Sea, is a busy commercial hub rich in spice trading. It is the centre of world spice trade for many centuries. It is emerging cosmopolitan city with busy life on streets, on the other hand it is also financial, commercial & industrial capital of Kerela. Its great to see that Kochi is the only city ot have underwater metro system which is world’s largest electric boat metro transportation infrastructure.

Alleppey – Kerala Backwaters & Houseboats

Alleppey, officially known as Alappuzha, famous among the travellers is also known as “Venice of East”. The is an immensely beautiful town, embraced by Arabian Sea in the west & many lakes, lagoons & freshwater rivers. Alleppey offers a unique experience with lush paddy fields, traditional houseboats in serene water & coconut groves.This town is also famous for its Nehru Trophy Boat Race where tourists come across the globe. This town has rich cultural heritage & natural beauty, which makes it a must visit destination for travellers which assures peace of mind amidst nature’s beauty.

Thekkady – Where Nature Rules

Located in the Idukki district of Kerela, Thekkady is a beautiful hill station adorned with lush greenery, amazing landscapes & diverse wildlife. Here is the famous Periyar National Park, which is a sanctuary that has variety of animals which are even exotic in their natural habitat. The beautiful Periyar lake which is located within the park have the facilities of boat cruises which offers travellers unique experience to watch wildlife along the water edges.

Munnar – Tea Plantations & Hill Landscapes

Munnar, the picturesque hill station of Kerala, is nestled in the Western Ghats and is renowned for its rolling tea plantations, mist-covered mountains, and breathtaking natural beauty. Often referred to as the 'Kashmir of South India', Munnar offers visitors a peaceful retreat surrounded by lush green valleys, waterfalls, and scenic landscapes. The region is home to sprawling tea estates, wildlife sanctuaries, and diverse flora and fauna, making it a paradise for nature lovers and photographers. Visitors can explore tea museums, enjoy panoramic viewpoints, and experience the serene atmosphere that makes Munnar one of South India's most sought-after travel destinations.

Mumbai – Colonial Heritage & Iconic Landmarks

Mumbai, famously known as the "City of Dreams", is India's financial capital and one of the country's most vibrant metropolitan cities. Located along the Arabian Sea, Mumbai is renowned for its iconic landmarks, diverse culture, colonial architecture, and dynamic lifestyle. The city perfectly blends modern skyscrapers with historic monuments, creating a unique urban experience. Visitors can explore famous attractions such as the Gateway of India, Marine Drive, Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Terminus, and the bustling local markets that showcase the city's energy and character. As the heart of India's film industry and a hub of culture, commerce, and entertainment, Mumbai offers unforgettable experiences for travelers seeking history, culture, and modern city life.

Day 1-3: Delhi

When you reach at the Delhi Airport, we’ll welcome you with a sign board and take you to your well-prepared hotel for a comfortable stay.

If you prefer to wake up early, you can explore more into Delhi’s rich history, politics, and spirituality. Explore everything from iconic landmarks such as India Gate, the President’s House, and Parliament House to the city’s major role in the Freedom Struggle bta Dive yourself in history at Gandhi Smriti and the unique Qutub Minar. Experience the charm of Old Delhi through a rickshaw ride, uncovering treasures such as Jama Masjid, Red Fort, and Raj Ghat. Don’t forget to explore Gurudwara Bangla Sahib and the serene Nizamuddin Sufi Saint Mosque.

Enrich your senses at Akshardham Temple, Humayun’s Tomb and Lotus Temple, and, and don’t miss the lively Chandni Chowk market for the perfect shopping.

We’ll manage your journey based on your schedule and preferences, inviting you to discover Delhi’s perfect mixture of history and modern vibrancy. Some places will be explored as per your convenience and based on your personal preferences and schedule.

Day 3-5: Jaipur

Jaipur, also known as the Pink city, is a major sightseeing destination in Rajasthan. Hawa mahal & Jal mahal are most famous iconic places to visit. You can explore the 7th-century Jantar Mantar which is an astronomical observatory. Discover the majestic Amber fort & enrich your eyes with its beauty. You can also visit the exquisite Birla Temple. Take a walk through the vibrant markets of Johri/Bapu Bazar and Choti Chopper, for the best shopping when you’re in India.

Day 6-7: Ajanta & Ellora

The Ajanta & Ellora Caves are UNESCO World Heritage Site world famous for ancient rock-cut temples & amazing cave paintings, which shows exceptional artistic & architectural achievements & breath-taking frescoes which date back to 2nd century BC.

Visit Bibi-Ka- Maqbara, the “Taj of the Deccan”, and have Mughal-inspired cuisine like Pulao and Biryani.

It is a world heritage site & historic railway station. It has great artwork of Victorian-Gothic style of architecture.

You can also visit Mani Bhawan, Haji Ali & Jain temple. You can alsi visit Dharavi, an area where famous movie shoot happens including Oscar-winning “Slumdog Millionaire”.

Day 10-11: Alleppey

Reach Alleppey by noon & check-in a well-booked beautiful houseboat. Enjoy boat cruise through mesmerising Vembanad Lake enjoying experience of backwater cruising.

As you travel narrow strips of land you can witness amazing life of livelihoods of fishing communities, women immersed in their daily lives & children swimming.

You’ll witness amazing spots along the way, feel free to explore them.

A scenic 3-hour and 30-minute drive, of 140 kilometres, to Thekkady

Day 11-12: Thekkady

Early morning you can go on a guided jungle hike & bamboo rafting at Periyar National Park. This will take around 6 hours & visiting there means exploring nature around & enjoying peaceful nature. There are many chances you get to witness the elephant herd in the jungle while hiking. After that you can enjoy Bamboo rafting on the Periyar Lake, where also you’ll have the chance to see wildlife and explore one of India’s exceptional animal sanctuaries.

You can also see fragrant spice market which has cinnamon, pepper & cardamom.

Venture into the fragrant spice plantations, where cardamom, cinnamon, pepper, and coffee thrive. At evening you can see kathakali Dance which is very famous for of Indian classical dance.

Next stop: Munnar

A 3-hour drive, covering 90 kilometres to Munnar.

Day 12-14: Munnar

Entering Munnar on the way you can witness world’s largest tea-growing region. After reaching you can go to see Kalaripayattu show which is an ancient martial art that originated in Kerala. Next day morning you can go to guided trek tour which will be 3-hour trekking tour covering 9km of walking which include grassland, tea plantations & rocks.

Next you can visit Munnar Tea Museum which preserve the aspects of the origin of tea plantation. Next you can also visit Pothamedu Viewpoint,a amazing spot which offers panoramic view of valley.

You can also visit Muthirapuzha River & Idukki Dam, the view from there is impressive which offers hill slopes view.

A 2-hour flight to Mumbai.

Reaching delhi you’ve two options, you can either relax ay your hotel for preparation of the departure of the next day.

Alternatively, if you’re feeling energetic and wish to explore more, Delhi also offers a variety of markets to visit: Chandni Chowk Market, the bustling Textile Market (Wholesale Market), the Book Market, and the attracting Spice Market.
